Privacy Policy

What we collect when you request a quote, how we use it and how to ask us to delete it.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information blainetreeremovals.com collects, how it is used and the choices you have. It applies to this website and to quote requests sent through it.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you send a quote request or message through our forms, we collect your name, phone number, ZIP code, the service you're interested in and anything you write in the message box. If you call, text or email us, we receive your phone number or email address and whatever you choose to tell us.

Information collected automatically. This site does not use analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not set its own cookies. Like any website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your request, give you a quote and schedule work
  • To contact you about a job you asked us about
  • To keep basic records of quotes and completed work

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to answer your request:

  • Formspree receives form submissions and forwards them to us by email. Its handling of data is described in Formspree's own privacy policy.
  • The tree service crew that performs the work, so they can contact you and do the job.
  • Google serves the fonts used on this site and the map on our homepage. When your browser loads them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information, as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Authorities, if we are required to by law.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and job records only as long as we need them to serve you, handle follow-up questions and meet record-keeping obligations, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what information we have about you, correct it or delete it. Call or text (651) 800-1205 or email tk8761767@gmail.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

Children

This site is meant for homeowners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page.

Contact
