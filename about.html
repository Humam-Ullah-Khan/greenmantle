A tree service that only works in Blaine

Blaine Tree Removals is a local tree service for homeowners in Blaine, Minnesota. We don't spread ourselves across the whole metro. Every job we take is in Blaine, which means shorter drive times, faster storm response and a crew that knows the trees, soils and city rules here.

Most of our work is tree removal: dead ash trees killed by emerald ash borer, trees leaning toward houses, and big trees in tight backyards that need careful rigging or a crane. We also handle stump grinding, trimming and pruning, and storm damage cleanup.

Fully insured

Tree work is dangerous, and a mistake can damage a roof, a fence or a neighbor's property. We carry insurance for the work we do and are happy to show proof of coverage before any job starts. If a tree service can't show you a certificate of insurance, don't let them climb your tree.

How our quotes work

  1. You call, text or send the form. Tell us what the tree is, where it is and what you'd like done. Photos help.
  2. We look at the tree in person. Size, condition, what's underneath it, access for equipment and whether it's near power lines all change how the job is done.
  3. You get a clear, written price. The quote is free and there's no obligation. It says exactly what's included: cutting, hauling, cleanup and stump grinding if you want it.
  4. We schedule the work and clean up after. Wood and brush are hauled away or left as firewood, whichever you prefer.

What we know about Blaine trees

  • Ash trees are dying. Emerald ash borer is established in Anoka County. Dead ash turns brittle quickly, so it's safer and usually cheaper to remove it early.
  • Oaks need careful timing. Pruning or removing oaks from April through July can spread oak wilt, so we schedule oak work in the dormant season when we can.
  • Boulevard trees belong to the city. Trees in the strip between the sidewalk and street are handled differently. We'll tell you when the City of Blaine needs to be involved.
  • Storms hit hard here. Summer thunderstorms and winter ice break limbs every year. For trees on houses, cars or driveways, we answer 24/7.
